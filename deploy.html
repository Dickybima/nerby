Before you deploy
Three things to check.

    
  • It costs real gas and cannot be undone. A deployed contract stays on the network forever. The fee shown is an estimate at the current gas price.
  • Read the source. The exact code being deployed is shown in the review panel. The token template uses OpenZeppelin contracts and sends the whole supply to your wallet.
  • Your wallet does the signing. This page and the Nerby Protocol server never see your keys. If the wallet shows a different network or amount than this page, reject it.
