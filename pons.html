How this works

What the ranking counts.

  • Volume across Pons venues. Each token’s 24h volume adds up its Pons bonding curve, the pool it graduated into, and older Pons pools. Trades on other DEXes are not counted, so rankings can differ from other lists.
  • On curve means the token still trades on its Pons bonding curve; the bar shows how close it is to graduating into a Uniswap V4 pool. Graduated tokens trade in that pool.
  • Market cap shows the reported market cap where available, otherwise the fully diluted value.
  • Scan before you buy. Open any token in Explore for supply, holders and a safety check. Nothing here is financial advice.
