Product

Every answer from the chain.

Nerby Protocol turns plain-language questions from your AI into live blockchain queries, and hands the results straight back.

01 · How it works

A question goes in. Chain data comes out.

Nerby Protocol is a small server that speaks MCP, the open standard AI apps use to call tools. It sits between your AI and the blockchain nodes.

Step 1

Connect

Add one URL to your AI client as an MCP server. No account, no API key, nothing to install.

Step 2

Ask

Ask in plain words. Your AI picks the right tool and Nerby Protocol queries a live node on the network you named.

Step 3

Decide

The answer comes back as structured data your AI can explain. For transactions and deployments you get a dry-run first — the decision and the signature stay with you.

02 · Tools

Fourteen tools. None of them can sign or send.

Your AI chooses among these on its own. The example prompts show the kind of question each one answers.

ToolWhat it doesTry asking
list_networksLists the chains you can query, with chain IDs and native currency.“Which networks can you reach?”
get_balanceNative balance of any address: ETH, POL or BNB.“How much ETH is in 0xd8dA…6045?”
get_token_balanceERC-20 balance of a holder, with the token symbol and decimals.“How much USDC does this wallet hold on Base?”
get_blockA block header: number, hash, time, gas used, base fee, transaction count.“What is the latest block on Arbitrum?”
get_transactionA transaction and its receipt: sender, recipient, value, status, fee, logs.“Did this transaction succeed, and what did it cost?”
get_logsEvent logs from a contract over a block range, up to 2,000 blocks per call.“Show recent Transfer events from this token.”
get_codeWhether an address is a contract, and how large its bytecode is.“Is this address a contract or a wallet?”
read_storageThe raw 32-byte value of any storage slot.“What is in slot 0 of this contract?”
call_contractCalls any read-only function from its Solidity signature and decodes the result.“Call totalSupply() on this token.”
get_gas_priceCurrent gas price and priority fee, in gwei.“What is gas on Ethereum right now?”
get_contract_sourceVerified source code and ABI of a contract, from Sourcify or Blockscout.“What does this contract do? Show me its functions.”
compile_contractCompiles Solidity (or the built-in token template) and reports errors, warnings and the ABI.“Does this contract compile?”
prepare_deploymentCompiles a contract, estimates the fee and returns a link to the deploy page, where you sign in your own wallet.“Create a token called Demo with 1,000,000 supply on Robinhood Chain.”
simulate_transactionDry-runs a transaction: success or revert reason, gas and fee estimate, and the unsigned transaction to review.“Would sending 0.1 ETH to this address go through?”
