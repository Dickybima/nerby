How trades work

You see it first. You sign it.

1 · Preview

Every command shows a preview

What you pay, what you get, the minimum you accept, price impact and fees. Nothing is sent until you confirm.

2 · Dry-run

Checked against the chain

Before your wallet opens, the exact transaction is simulated on Robinhood Chain. If it would fail, it is stopped and nothing is sent.

3 · Your wallet

Signed by you

Your browser wallet shows the transaction and you approve it there. Nerby Protocol never sees your keys and adds no fee.

  • Where trades go. Buys and sells go straight to the $NERBY bonding curve on Pons V2 (0xc672…906c). The curve charges 1% protocol fee plus 1.8% creator tax on every trade; Nerby Protocol charges nothing.
  • Slippage protects you. Each trade carries a minimum amount. If the price moves more than your slippage before it lands, the curve cancels it.
  • Sending is final. Transfers and burns cannot be reversed. Check the address before you confirm.
  • After graduation. When the curve sells out, $NERBY moves to a Uniswap V4 pool and the terminal will stop trading on the curve.
  • Not financial advice. Trading carries risk; only use what you can afford to lose.
