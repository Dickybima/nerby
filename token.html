Good to know

What these numbers mean.

  • Price, market cap, liquidity and volume come from the $NERBY / WETH pool via GeckoTerminal and refresh every minute. They can lag the chain by a minute or two.
  • Burned means sent to the burn address. Nobody holds its key, so those tokens can never move again. The contract’s total supply figure stays at one billion.
  • The buyback wallet is found automatically as the address that sends the most $NERBY to the burn address; buybacks are its purchases from the pool.
  • This is not financial advice. $NERBY does not unlock any feature of Nerby Protocol today, and the product is free to use.
